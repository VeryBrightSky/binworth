Questions

Is this real?

The app isn't out yet. The figure prices are real, sourced and dated, and you can tap any price in the gallery to check it.

When does it come out?

No date yet. We'll let people into the app a few at a time, starting with whoever joined first. You'll get one email when it's your turn.

What do I get for joining?

Early access to the app (the earlier you join, the sooner you get it) and 10 free bin scans. Joining is free.

What's a bin scan?

You take one photo of a pile of LEGO. The app finds the minifigures and valuable pieces and tells you what the whole bin is worth. One photo is one scan.

How do I get the app sooner?

After you join, you get your own link. Every friend who joins with it moves you up 10 spots. Bonuses: 1 friend gets you 5 more free scans, 3 friends puts you in the very first group to get the app, and 5 friends gets you 50 free scans. Fake or duplicate signups don't count.

Which figures should I look for?

Start with the figures from our videos. Older Star Wars, Batman, Ninjago and Minecraft figures are worth checking first. Each one has a "look for" line so you can tell it apart from the common versions.

Where do the prices come from?

The figure values on this page come from BrickEconomy and from Brickset used averages based on BrickLink sales, checked Nov 7 and 8, 2026. The app will price bins from recent sold prices for used LEGO, not store prices and not guesses.

iPhone or Android?

We're starting with iPhone, with Android planned next. Join and we'll tell you when yours is ready.

We use it to tell you when the app launches, and that's it. We never sell it. You can ask us to delete it anytime at hello@pricemybin.com. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
