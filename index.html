Questions

How much is a bin of LEGO worth?

Loose mixed LEGO usually sells for about $5 to $12 a pound on eBay (2026 sold listings), so a 20 lb bin is roughly $100 to $240 as bricks. The money is in the minifigures: they sell one by one, and a single rare figure like the 2003 Cloud City Boba Fett ($1,684 used) or chrome gold C-3PO ($1,193 used) can be worth more than the whole bin of bricks. PriceMyBin will find those figures from one photo. Until it launches, check the 86 figures above against your bin.

What do I get for joining?

Early access to the app (the earlier you join, the sooner you get it) and 10 free bin scans. Joining is free.

What's a bin scan?

You take one photo of a pile of LEGO. The app finds the minifigures and valuable pieces and tells you what the whole bin is worth. One photo is one scan.

How do I get the app sooner?

After you join, you get your own link. Every friend who joins with it moves you up 10 spots. Fake or duplicate signups don't count.

Can I sell my figures to a shop instead of listing them?

Yes, that's the plan. For each valuable figure the app will show two choices: sell it yourself for the full used price, or get a cash offer from a LEGO shop that buys by mail. The shop pays less than you'd get selling it yourself, but you skip the listing, shipping and waiting. You always see the full price first, and the choice is yours. If a shop buys your figures, PriceMyBin may earn a fee from the shop. That never changes the prices we show you.

Which figures should I look for?

Start with the figures from our videos. Older Star Wars, Batman, Ninjago and Lord of the Rings figures are worth checking first. Each one has a "look for" line so you can tell it apart from the common versions.

Where do the prices come from?

Every figure price on this page is a Brickset used average based on real BrickLink sales, checked Nov 9, 2026. The app will price bins from recent sold prices for used LEGO, not store prices and not guesses.
