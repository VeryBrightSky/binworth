Why a bin is worth more than it looks

Most bins get priced by eye or by the pound. The money is usually in a handful of pieces.

$3 to $12

what loose used LEGO usually sells for per pound, depending on the mix.

1 figure

can be worth more than the rest of the bin. Older Star Wars and Harry Potter figures are the usual suspects.

2 to 4x

what an unsorted bin can bring once the figures and sets are pulled and sold on their own.
