Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version

  • If you join the waitlist, we keep your email, one optional answer, and where you came from so we can tell you when the app launches.
  • We don't sell your information, we don't use ad trackers, and we don't share it for advertising. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites.
  • You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your information at any time: email hello@pricemybin.com.

1. Who we are

PriceMyBin ("PriceMyBin," "we," "us") runs pricemybin.com. PriceMyBin is a small, independent project operated by a sole proprietor in Pennsylvania, USA. We're building an app that estimates what a bin of used LEGO® is worth from a photo. The app is not out yet. Right now this website shows LEGO figure values from public price guides, a few example bins, and a free waitlist.

Questions about this policy go to hello@pricemybin.com.

2. What we collect

When you join the waitlist

WhatWhy
Your email addressTo tell you when the app launches
Your answer to "What are you doing with LEGO?" (optional: flipping bins, selling old LEGO, or just curious)To decide which features to build first
If you fix a typo in your email after joining, a note saying which address it replacesSo we can delete the wrong address
Where you came from: a short code from the link you used (like "tt" for TikTok), the name of the website that linked you, or "direct". Also your own short invite code, and the invite code of the friend whose link you used, if anyTo see which places bring people to the waitlist, and to move you up the list when friends join from your link

We do not ask for your name, phone number, home address, birthday, payment details, or location.

Stored only on your device

The site saves two small notes in your browser's local storage: one remembers that you joined, so we stop asking you to, and one keeps your best score in the "Would you flip it?" game. While your browser tab is open, it also remembers which link brought you here, so it can be included if you join. After you join, it also saves your short invite code so it can show your spot in line; the site looks up that spot by code, never by email. These notes stay on your device unless you join, and you can clear them anytime in your browser settings. We do not set cookies.

Collected by the services that run the site

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Forms (Google LLC) receives and stores waitlist sign-ups for us, along with standard technical details of the request. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We count visits two ways, both without cookies. Our own first-party counter notes which parts of the page you reach and which buttons you tap, and sends that to a server we run on Cloudflare; it never stores your IP address, and the visitor ID it uses is a hash of your IP and browser that changes every day, so you can't be followed across days or sites. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.) to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't fingerprint your device, and doesn't follow you to other sites. It receives standard technical details such as your IP address, browser and the page you viewed. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. We don't use advertising pixels or social media trackers. Pages on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube that we link to are covered by those companies' own privacy policies.

3. How we use your information

  • To email you when PriceMyBin launches, including how to claim the free scans offered to waitlist members.
  • To understand who the app is for and where our sign-ups come from.
  • To keep the site and waitlist working and to block spam or abuse.
  • To follow the law and respond to lawful requests.

We won't send you other marketing emails unless you ask for them. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only:

  • With service providers that run things for us: Google (Forms and storage), GitHub (hosting), and, at launch, the email service we use to send the launch email. They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • If the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of PriceMyBin, our users or others.
  • If PriceMyBin is sold or merged, the waitlist may move to the new owner, who must keep honoring this policy for it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If the app launches and you don't create an account, we delete your waitlist entry within 12 months of launch. If we stop the project, we delete the waitlist.

6. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we have about you and give you a copy,
  • correct it,
  • delete it, or
  • stop emailing you (you can also use the unsubscribe link in any email).

Email hello@pricemybin.com from the address you signed up with. We'll reply within 30 days. If we can't do something you ask, we'll explain why, and you can reply to ask us to reconsider. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Your California privacy rights

California residents can request the information above. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. People in other states with privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Virginia and others) can use the same process.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We don't track you across other websites, so there is no cross-site tracking for these signals to turn off. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a request not to sell or share your information, which we already don't do.

If you're in the EU, UK or another country with similar laws

We use your waitlist information because you chose to give it to us (your consent). You can withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Your information is stored in the United States.

7. Children

PriceMyBin is not meant for children under 13, and you must be 13 or older to join the waitlist (16 or older if you live in the EU or UK). We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you're a parent or guardian and think your child gave us information, email hello@pricemybin.com and we'll delete it.

8. Security

The site runs over HTTPS, and only the PriceMyBin owner can see the waitlist. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a breach ever puts your information at risk, we'll notify you as the law requires.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how we use the information you already gave us, we'll email the waitlist before it takes effect.
